Act II
Long before Satania, Dei stood below the heavens as the city of God, a place where memories rested untouched by time. It was high above that Aether Luminous was born. What should have been the life of a blessed angel instead became one shaped by loss, agony, and sorrow. As the pressures of faith, duty, and affairs begin to wear him down, Aether is forced onto a path that slowly turns the Radiant Knight into deep tragedy.

After the events of His Machiavellian Grace, Satania is left divided by fear, political unrest, and the growing influence of Yose. Monika Iris and Aether Luminous find themselves on opposite sides of a conflict neither of them fully understands, yet both are trapped within. Between failing benediction, personal loss, and malediction, the city continues to unravel, the future of Satania depends not only on the demon but on he who walks before her, the angel.
